Four small calculators expose the equations used in the chapters. These are accounting models, not hardware benchmarks or fitted predictions. Values are recomputed entirely in your browser.

1. The compute–bandwidth balance

Runtime is bounded below by $\max(F/P,Q/\beta)$. The model assumes ideal overlap and omits launch, synchronization, and other overhead. Read Lecture 2.

2. How much memory does the history need?

KV cache is $2LBTH_{kv}d_hb$ bytes. This excludes weights, workspaces, metadata, and any special cache compression. Read Lecture 10.

3. A fixed budget has to fund both model and data

For $L=L_\infty+AN^{-\alpha}+BD^{-\beta}$ and $C=\kappa ND$, the optimum scales as $N_*\propto C^{\beta/(\alpha+\beta)}$ and $D_*\propto C^{\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)}$. This assumes fixed coefficients, recipe, and regime. Read Lecture 9.

4. Near-duplicate retrieval is probabilistic

With independent MinHash rows, candidate probability is $1-(1-s^r)^b$. Candidates still need a removal policy and, often, a similarity check. Read Lecture 14.
